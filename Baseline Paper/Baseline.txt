# Three Baselines
1. EAYB: Mobile proxy caching adapted to LEO, DIBR synthesis, no ISL cooperation
(M. Yeh, C. -H. Wang, D. -N. Yang, J. -T. Lee and W. Liao, "Mobile Proxy Caching for Multi-View 3D Videos With Adaptive View Selection," in IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, vol. 21, no. 8, pp. 2909-2921, 1 Aug. 2022)

2. SCA+EAYB: EAYB + satellite-based cooperative caching with K-means clustering
(S. Bhandari, T. X. Vu and S. Chatzinotas, "User-Centric Flexible Resource Management Framework for LEO Satellites With Fully Regenerative Payload," in IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, vol. 42, no. 5, pp. 1246-1261, May 2024)

3. RFP+EAYB: EAYB + ridge regression prediction for regional content placement
(J. Tang et al., "Cooperative Caching in Satellite-Terrestrial Integrated Networks: A Region Features Aware Approach," in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, vol. 73, no. 7, pp. 10602-10616, July 2024)
